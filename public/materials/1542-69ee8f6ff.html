14.10.2014 - 13:53

German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) announced a competition for DAAD scholarship programmes for 2015/2016 academic year.

Detailed information as well as application forms could be found atwww.daad.org.ua. Applications will be handed in to the DAAD information centre in Kiev.

For information and advice about training and studies in Germany please call (044) 406-82-69, (044) 406-85-41 or go to DAAD information centre.
