01.02.2016 (All day)

Dear Colleagues

We invite you and your colleagues to visit our International Scientific Conference“Ukraine in the modern world: challenges and opportunities”,which will take place in Kiev from 13 th till 14 th of April 2016.

Conference is organized by: Federation of Trade Unions of Ukraine,International Association of academic and research institutions of training for social and labour sphere, The Academy of Labour, Social Relations and Tourism, Janusz Korczak Pedagogical University in Warsaw.

Сonference topics:

  • Section 1. Protection of rights and freedoms of citizens under present conditions.
  • Section 2.Economic aspects of Ukraine: a paradigm shift.
  • Section 3.Social and human problems of modern Ukraine.
  • Section 4.Foreign languages and information and communication technologies for socio-economic development of the country.

The working languages of the conference – Ukrainian, Russian, English.

We request you to provide us with the names and surnames of all members of your delegation until 10 th of February іn advance to prepare the certificates of participation.

The organizational fee for all participants of the Conference is 150 UAH (equivalent 6 USD)for two days.For accommodation you can book rooms in the Academy hostel, which have 12 comfortable double rooms. You can also stay in other more comfortable hotels in Kiev.

We ask you to let us know in advance if you plan to attend the Conference, by sending e-mail info@socosvita.kiev.uaor call to +38 044 522 49 40 (contact person is Irina Halahuz).

The address of the holding the Conference is: Kyiv, Velyka Okruzhna doroga, 3 (the metro station: “TEREMKI”) The Academy of Labour, Social Relations and Tourism.
