POST-GRADUATE STUDIES

  • Second degree
  • Further education without a degree
  • Further education of specialists.

POST-GRADUATE RESEARCH

Post-graduate research student receive education in the following branches:

  • Economy and operation of business (in accordance with economic activities).
  • Land law; agrarian law; ecolaw; natural resources law
  • Administrative law and process; financial law; legal aspects of computing.
  • Theory and history of sociology

ADDITIONAL EDUCATION

Additional education of students, graduates and other persons in order to enhance their competitiveness at the labour market and meeting their other requirements:

  • Program “Facultatives” (with registration of the results in the diploma annex)
  • Certificate programmes (additional contemporary courses and master-classes with handing out a certificate)
  • Deeper learning of foreign languages
  • Military department, driving school.
