Workshop ‘Tools to support mobility of researchers are among US priorities for the year 2014 through 2020’

← Усі матеріали
Повний текст матеріалу

10.06.2015 - 12:19

On June 9 the Academy held a workshop ‘Tools to support mobility of researchers are among US priorities for the year 2014 through 2020’. Organizers of the event were Central European Academy Studies and Certification (CEASC) foundation, University of Economy in Bydgoszcz (WSG), Robert Schuman European Centre (CERS) and Europe Direct Information Service Network.

More than 300 attendants learned about participation of Ukrainian educational establishments and other institutions in European programmes, such as Horizon2020, Marie Sklodowska-Curie and Erasmus+ (structure of programmes, conditions of participation, competition documentation, Participant Portal, information about current competitions, etc.) and discussed the role of postgraduate studies in contemporary European educational space.
