11.11.2014 - 13:48

It is a joint educational project of Warsaw Janusz Korczak Pedagogical University and the Academy of labour and social relations the aim of which is to offer the highest level of training for the future experts in the field of social project financing. The students enjoyed the opportunity to attend during a year lectures and seminars in both Kiev and Warsaw

Now the students are looking forward to the final stage of the programme – public presentation of their diploma thesis in December.
