Academy of Labour, Social Relations and Tourism and Janusz Korczak Pedagogical University in Warsaw invite toPostgraduate studies “Master of Social Administration” (MSA)

Course aims: preparation of experts for Ukraine for acquiring European Union funds and become project managers; graduates equipped with Polish postgraduate degree according to Ministry Standards and Double Degree will have competitive advantage when seeking employment in Ukraine and as well in Ukraine market; Janusz Korczak Pedagogical University in Warsaw will provide internships in Polish companies for all participants of the course.

  • 2 semesters (1-year), 180 – 200 contact hours;
  • 40 percent in e-learning; 60 percent in traditional lectures (in Poland and in Ukraine); mixed staff – Polish/Ukrainian; students will come to Poland for about 3-4 weeks on traineeship; Polish lecturers will come to Ukraine;
  • Language of instruction: English (partly lectures will be provided in Polish by Polish);
  • Final diploma: (1) joint MSA Certificate of Academy of Labor, Social Relations and Tourism; (2) official post-graduate Polish degree issued on Polish Ministry of Higher Education paper;
  • Planned group: 20 students and more (it will be additional course for students; students of all faculties of ALSRT can apply; English level should be at least B1 – self-assessment – no exams);
  • Title: “European Integration – Economic and Political Aspects – modernization through EU projects”.

Curriculum:

  • European integration
  • law of European union
  • globalization and world development
  • institutions of European union
  • eastern partnership
  • methodology of project writing
  • polish language
  • European social funds
  • entrepreneurship and social innovation
  • European social policy
  • cross-cultural management
  • spin off/spin out companies
  • social policy in Ukraine
  • project management
  • corporate social responsibility
  • workshops on preparing social projects

Requirements for entrants: degree "bachelor" in humanitarian areas.

Tuition fees: 1100 €
