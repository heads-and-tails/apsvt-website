To students: The Government of the United States of America announces the 2015-2016 Global Undergraduate Exchange Program

  • Головна
  • ›
  • Календар подій

09.02.2015 - 13:43

The Global UGRAD scholarship provides one academic semester of undergraduate, non-degree study in the United States in all fields except medicine and law.

This international educational and cultural exchange program supports talented international students with the application process to U.S. higher education institutions, brings future leaders to the United States to experience the U.S. educational system, enhance their knowledge, and explore U.S. culture and values.

Please follow the link for eligibility requirements and application form: http://goo.gl/9eNiFn

For more information, please contact at: KyivUGRAD@state.gov and by phone: +38 044 521 50 43

Application Deadline: Sunday, February 22, 2015
