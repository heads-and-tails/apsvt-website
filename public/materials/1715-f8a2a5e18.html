German university has become a partner of the Academy

  • Головна
  • ›
  • Календар подій

07.12.2015 - 09:40

One of the largest private schools in Germany - The School of International Business and Entrepreneurship (SIBE) - recently signed a cooperation agreement with the Academy. In partnership joint educational programs will be developed and implemented. Now the partners are developing international educational project which extends the competence of specialists in law.

_The School of International Business and Entrepreneurship(SIBE)is the international Business School of the Steinbeis University Berlin, the largest private university with the right to award doctorates in Germany. Around 3,000 students have been globally qualified in job-integrated study programs. These management programs ensure the individual learning success of each participant and a direct benefit to currently 350 partner companies._
