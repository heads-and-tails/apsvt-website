For students: International Exchange Program 2013-2014

Повний текст матеріалу

30.08.2013 - 21:48

Ministry announces the start of the International Exchange Program 2013-2014, organized by the International Organization AIESEC.

As part of the exchange program a set of participants conducted among students of higher educational establishments of Ukraine in three areas:

  • Culture (for undergraduate majors: cultural studies, sociology, ethnology, anthropology, tourism, education, philology and history);
  • Education (for undergraduate majors: economics, education, marketing, finance, linguistics, sociology, IT);
  • The world's problems (for undergraduate majors: sociology, psychology, medicine, social sciences, international relations, law).

The purpose of the exchange program is to enable young people to develop responsible leadership with global thinking through intercultural living and working environment.

The project takes place in two stages: in winter training - in the period from 15/12/2013 to 25/03/2014, for summer internship - in the period from 05/15/2014 to 15/09/2014. Duration of internship is from 6 to 8 weeks.

For more details read the information about exchange programs on the website: www.aiesec.ua
